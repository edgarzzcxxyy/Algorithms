## Interviewer notes (gas station session)

- Graph problems: always clarify directed vs undirected. It decides how the graph is built; for a directed graph, whether it has cycles also matters.
- Asking "what if the car can never reach the end?" was a good clarification. Define the return value yourself; -1 is the usual choice.
- Common pattern: plain BFS/DFS with one extra variable in the state (here fuel). Put it in both the queue entry and the visited set; everything else is standard BFS.
- Follow-up: with vs without pruning, and the time/space complexity of each.
